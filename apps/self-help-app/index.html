Self-Help App

Self-insight without questionnaires — one short, ordinary scenario a day, and a growth profile that builds itself from how you actually respond.

Getting started

  1. Tell it what you're working on — a one-time onboarding asks about the area of life you want to grow in. That's the only setup.
  2. Take today's scenario — a brief, everyday situation (a queue, a text message, a decision at work) with a few natural ways to respond.
  3. Choose honestly — there is no right answer and no score on the screen. The scenario is the whole exercise.
  4. Watch the Growth tab — over days, your choices quietly assemble a picture of your habits, strengths, and blind spots.

The idea behind it

Ask someone "are you patient?" and you get who they'd like to be. Watch how they handle a slow queue and you get who they are. This app is built on that gap: instead of quizzes and self-ratings, it offers small fictional situations and reads the pattern in your natural responses — an approach drawn from research into indirect psychological assessment.

Because the scenarios are generated fresh each day around your chosen goal, the practice stays alive instead of becoming a form to fill in.

Common questions

Is this therapy? No. It's a reflection practice — a mirror, not treatment. If you're struggling, please reach out to a qualified professional; the app is a complement to self-work, never a substitute for care.
Can I "game" the scenarios? You can — but you'd only be describing the person you want to appear to be, which is itself worth noticing. The practice pays back exactly the honesty you put in.
Why can't I see how choices are scored? Deliberately. The moment responses feel scored, people perform instead of respond. The Growth tab shows you the accumulated picture, not per-answer marks.
