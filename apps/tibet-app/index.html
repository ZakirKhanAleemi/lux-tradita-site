Rabjung

The Rabjung cycle — the elements and animals of the Tibetan almanac, kept for your day.

How it works

  • The Sixty-Year Cycle — Each year, day and hour pairs one of five elements with one of twelve animals — a quality and a force. The rabjung turns through all sixty combinations before it repeats, shown on a live wheel.
  • Mewa and Parkha — The nine mewa of the magic square and the eight parkha trigrams place the day in the deeper Tibetan reckoning of element and fortune, alongside the day-sign.
  • A Daily Practice, Hidden in a Story — Each day's element and animal set the quality and bring one short, true-to-life situation with three choices. Your choices gather on a growth star that records a lifetime of practice. No scores to chase.
  • Ask Anything — Ask about today's element and animal, your birth signs, a mewa, or the tradition itself — the current calendar and your birth details are already in view. Plain language, any time.
  • Add Your Birth Date — Your birth year, element and animal colour every reading. Optional — and your birth details stay on your device.

Getting started

  1. Open the Today tab — see the day's element and animal in the sixty-year rabjung cycle.
  2. Take the daily practice — a short scenario carrying today's quality. There are no wrong answers.
  3. Enter your birth date in Settings to reveal your birth element, animal and mewa.
  4. Watch your Growth profile take shape from your responses over the days.

The tradition behind it

Tibetan astrology (nagtsi, elemental calculation) reads time through five elements and twelve animals turning in a sixty-year cycle — the rabjung — together with the nine mewa numbers and the eight parkha trigrams. The app computes the Nagtsi calendar on your device; it does not simulate Kartsi (planetary astronomy), which needs its own ephemeris.

Common questions

Is this the same as the Chinese zodiac? They share the twelve animals and five elements, but the Tibetan system has its own calendar, the mewa and parkha, and its own way of reading a person's life force, body, power, luck and soul.
Does it work offline? The calendar, your birth signs and the mewa are computed on the device. The daily scenario and Ask need a connection to our generation service.
