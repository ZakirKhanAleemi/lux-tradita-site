This data lives in the app's local storage on your device. It is not uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

What leaves your device

To generate your daily scenario and answer your questions, the app sends a prompt to our generation server. The prompt can include elements derived from your data (for example, your current planetary period or the day's reading) so the response is personal to you.

  • Prompts are processed to generate the response and are not used to build profiles of you.
  • Our server may pass the prompt to an AI provider (e.g. Anthropic) to generate the text, subject to that provider's data policies, or generate it with models we run ourselves.
  • No account, name, or device identifier is attached to these requests.

Notifications

Morning notifications are opt-in, scheduled and composed entirely on your device, and can be turned off at any time in Settings. Enabling them requires your operating system's notification permission.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by your device's app store (Google Play or the App Store). We never see or store your payment details.

Data deletion

The apps published by Lux Tradita do not create accounts and do not require sign-in. Every piece of personal data you enter is stored only on your device.

  • Delete all your data for any app in the list above by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling permanently and irreversibly erases everything the app stored on your device.
  • We hold no server-side account or profile tied to you, so there is nothing for us to retain after you uninstall.
  • If you would like us to confirm this, or to request deletion of any data you believe we may hold, email support@luxtradita.com from the address you wish to enquire about and we will respond within 30 days.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no passwords, no email collection.
  • No advertising, no trackers, no third-party analytics SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information.

Children

These apps are not directed at children under 13 and provide no child-specific features.

Important disclaimer

Readings and scenarios are for reflection and personal growth. They are not medical, psychological, financial, or legal advice, and not a predictive science. The classical traditions themselves warn against deterministic use.
